

| Project Name | Zapas: AI-Based Inventory Demand Forecasting and Customer Review Analysis for Restocking Decision Support  |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Domain** |  Retail Inventory Management / Customer Feedback Analytics  |
| **Problem Statement** | Retail businesses may face difficulties in making effective restocking decisions when relying only on historical sales and current inventory levels without considering customer feedback. Products with high sales may still receive negative reviews related to quality, which can lead to repeated customer dissatisfaction if the same products are restocked without improvement. Therefore, Zapas aims to integrate AI-based demand forecasting and customer review analysis to help businesses predict inventory needs, identify common product issues, and provide recommendations for better restocking and product improvement decisions.   |
| **Evidence of the Problem** | Evidence 1: **Challenges in Retail Demand Forecasting** Source / citation: Fildes et al. (2022) highlighted that retail demand forecasting is challenging because customer demand is influenced by various factors, including promotions and product-level variations. This demonstrates the importance of accurate demand forecasting to support inventory planning and restocking decisions. Evidence 2: **Evidence 2: Importance of Customer Reviews for Product Improvement**  Source / citation: Zhang et al. (2022) demonstrated that online customer reviews can be analysed to identify customer needs and support product improvement strategies. Their findings show that sentiment analysis can help businesses understand customer feedback and identify product attributes that require improvement.  |
| **Initial Related Work (at least 3\)** | Study / solution 1: **Retail Sales Forecasting** Source / citation: Makridakis et al. (2022) investigated retail sales forecasting using different forecasting methods, including machine learning, through the M5 forecasting competition. Key limitation: The study focused on forecasting accuracy rather than combining demand predictions with customer complaints to support product quality and restocking decisions. Study / solution 2: **Customer Review Analysis for Product Improvement** Source / citation: Zhang et al. (2022) proposed a framework that analyses positive, negative, and neutral customer reviews to identify customer requirements and support product improvement strategies. Key limitation: The framework focused on identifying product improvement opportunities without integrating inventory demand forecasting or restocking recommendations. Study / solution 3: **Sales Forecasting Using Customer Reviews**  Source / citation: Fan et al. (2017) developed a sales forecasting method that combines historical sales data with sentiment analysis of online customer reviews. Key limitation: The study focused on improving sales forecasting using automotive industry data rather than providing integrated recommendations for inventory replenishment and product quality improvement.  |
| **Engineering Gap / Limitation** | Existing approaches often focus on either inventory demand forecasting or customer review analysis separately. Although some studies combine sales data and customer sentiment to improve forecasting accuracy, they do not necessarily provide integrated recommendations for restocking and product quality improvement. As a result, businesses may struggle to determine whether high-demand products should be restocked immediately or reviewed for quality issues first. Zapas aims to address this gap by integrating demand forecasting, customer sentiment analysis, and complaint identification into a single decision-support system that helps businesses make more informed restocking and product improvement decisions. |
| **Initial Project Objectives (2–3)** | O1: To design an AI-based decision support system that integrates inventory demand forecasting and customer review analysis for retail businesses. O2: To develop the system using machine learning to predict product demand and natural language processing to identify customer sentiment and common product complaints, providing recommendations for restocking and product improvement. O3: To evaluate the system's performance in terms of demand forecasting accuracy, sentiment classification performance, complaint identification accuracy, and the relevance of generated recommendations.  |
| **Target Users** | The target users of Zapas are retail business owners, inventory managers, and product management teams. The system helps inventory managers predict future stock requirements, while business owners and product management teams can analyse customer feedback, identify common product issues, and make informed decisions regarding restocking and product quality improvement. |

These reviews will be linked to products in the sales dataset to demonstrate how Zapas performs sentiment analysis, identifies common product complaints, and provides restocking and product improvement recommendations. |
| **Similar to Sage and Yotpo** | Zapas shares similarities with existing applications such as Inventory Planner by Sage and Yotpo Insights. Inventory Planner uses historical sales and inventory data to forecast product demand and support restocking decisions, while Yotpo Insights uses natural language processing (NLP) to analyse customer reviews, identify sentiment, and extract common product issues. Similarly, Zapas aims to provide demand forecasting, customer review analysis, and useful insights to help retail businesses make better inventory and product management decisions.  |
| **Different from Sage and Yotpo**  | Unlike Inventory Planner by Sage, which primarily focuses on demand forecasting and inventory replenishment, and Yotpo Insights, which focuses on customer review analysis, Zapas aims to integrate both functions into a single decision support system. Zapas will consider predicted product demand, available stock, and customer feedback when generating restocking recommendations. For example, if a product has high predicted demand but receives many negative reviews about its quality, the system can recommend investigating and improving the product before restocking in large quantities. This allows businesses to consider both inventory requirements and customer satisfaction when making decisions.  |
| **Responsible AI Risks** | Risk 1: **Inaccurate Demand Forecasting** The AI model may produce inaccurate demand predictions due to limited historical sales data or changing customer purchasing patterns. This could lead businesses to overstock or understock products, resulting in financial losses or missed sales opportunities. Risk 2: **Misinterpretation of Customer Reviews** The NLP model may incorrectly classify customer sentiment or fail to identify the actual issues mentioned in reviews, especially when reviews contain sarcasm or mixed opinions. This could lead to inappropriate product improvement recommendations and poor business decisions. Risk 3: **Synthetic Data Bias** Synthetic customer reviews may not accurately represent real customer opinions or purchasing experiences. Relying heavily on synthetic data could produce misleading sentiment patterns and recommendations, affecting the reliability of the system.  |
